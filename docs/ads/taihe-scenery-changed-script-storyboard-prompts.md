# 太和工房「風景換了，它沒換」— 劇本 · 分鏡 · 生成 Prompt

搭配《導演拍攝設定書》（同資料夾）使用。設定書管「怎麼拍」，這份管「拍什麼」與「餵給 AI 什麼」。

---

# 一、劇本（30s，無對白）

## 場 1 · 河濱自行車道 · 日出前 · 外景

黑畫面。一聲「喀」，瓶蓋打開。

畫面亮起：瓶蓋特寫，拇指還壓在蓋上，背後是還沒亮透的藍色天光。

切。她站在河堤上，剛跑完，手撐著膝蓋直起身。右手握著壺，壺站在畫面右邊。她看一眼前面的路，喝兩口，很快，喘還沒停。遠處天空從藍轉橘。

切。大遠景。她從畫面左邊跑向右邊，河濱燈柱一根一根過去。

## 場 2 · 公車窗邊 · 上午 · 陰 · 內景

公車裡。她靠窗坐著，窗外街景灰白地流過去。她看著窗外，但沒有在看。

切。同一個構圖，壺在同一個位置。她開蓋、喝兩口、蓋回，眼睛一直沒離開窗。動作全自動。「喀」很輕。

## 場 3 · 山徑觀景點 · 中午 · 陰 · 外景

低角度。她走到觀景點停下來。山谷在她面前打開，白霧在山腰。

切。同一個構圖。她先看風景，然後笑出來，是給風景的笑。大口喝，嘴角有水。

切。特寫。她用手背把嘴角的水擦掉，還在笑。

## 場 4 · 北海岸礁岩 · 日落前 · 風 · 外景

長焦。她站在礁岩上背對太陽，頭髮被風吹起，慢動作。浪在她後面碎掉。

切。同一個構圖。她很慢地開蓋，喝一口，蓋子沒有馬上蓋回去。她看海。

## 連環

壺沒有動。背景每三分之一秒換一次：河濱、公車、山、海、河濱、山、公車、海。她的臉、衣服、頭髮、光線一直在跳。壺一直在同一個地方。

最後一次跳——落進夜雨。

## 場 5 · 城市騎樓 · 夜 · 雨 · 外景

她站在騎樓底下躲雨，霓虹在濕地上反光。她沒有喝。兩手抱著壺靠在胸口。她看著雨從騎樓邊掉下來，一滴一滴。深呼吸一次。鏡頭手持，慢慢靠近她。音樂只剩一台鋼琴。

（F60 版：她把臉貼在壺上，壺是溫的。TR55 版：手指在壺身上輕輕敲。）

## 場 6 · 家 · 餐桌 · 夜 · 內景

鏡頭第一次完全不動。

餐桌，一顆檯燈。她坐下，把壺放在桌上，壺落在畫面右邊同一個位置。手停在壺上一秒。放開。

她起身，椅子拖一聲，走出畫面。

畫面左邊，她的臉一直待的地方，空了。

字淡入：

**風景換了，它沒換。**

## 結尾

太和工房 Logo。TR55 運動水壺 / ST-CBH-F60 保溫瓶。

（選用旁白，只在字出現時說一句：「還在。」）

## 分鏡草圖要點（給畫分鏡或給 AI 出草圖）

- 每張 C 格：畫面分三直條。左條是臉（側 3/4 朝右），中條是背景，右條是一支直立的壺，瓶蓋頂貼上 1/3 橫線。
- 六張 C 格排在一起看，壺要能用一把尺畫一條線連過去。
- 6C 多畫一張「她離開後」的空格：左條空、中條檯燈餐桌、右條壺在桌上，字在左條。

# 三、生成 Prompt 總包

給 AI 圖像 / 影片模型用（Higgsfield、Veo、Kling、Runway、Midjourney 皆適用）。流程建議：

1. 先生成**角色參考表**與**產品參考圖**（或直接上傳實品照當 reference）。
2. 每顆鏡頭先出**關鍵幀圖**（image prompt），確認構圖後再用 image-to-video 出動態（video prompt）。
3. C 鏡頭全部用同一張「構圖底圖」當 reference，只換背景描述，這是「壺位不變」在 AI 端的做法。
4. 產品外觀一律用實品照 reference，不要靠文字描述；文字只負責位置與光。

## 3.1 共用區塊（每個 prompt 前面貼）

**STYLE（風格）**
```
Cinematic commercial film still, shot on full-frame cinema camera, 50mm lens, f/2.8, natural light with subtle product fill, 24fps motion blur, 10-bit log graded, Taiwanese contemporary lifestyle advertising, restrained and quiet, no text, no logo overlays, no watermark, photorealistic, 16:9.
```

**CHARACTER（女主，全片一致）**
```
A Taiwanese woman in her late twenties, shoulder-length black hair, natural minimal makeup, calm expressive face, slim build, holding a water bottle in her RIGHT hand with four fingers wrapped around it and thumb in front, elbow slightly bent, bottle held one fist away from her chest. Same person in every shot.
```

**PRODUCT — TR55（依實品修正顏色與蓋型）**
```
TAIHE TR55 sports water bottle, 550ml, [COLOR] translucent body, water visible sloshing inside, [flip-top / screw] lid, brand logo facing camera, clean surface, no fingerprints. Use the attached product photo as strict visual reference; do not redesign the bottle.
```

**PRODUCT — ST-CBH-F60（依實品修正）**
```
TAIHE ST-CBH-F60 stainless steel vacuum insulated bottle, 600ml, brushed silver body, screw cap, brand logo facing camera, controlled soft reflections, no crew or camera visible in reflection. Use the attached product photo as strict visual reference; do not redesign the bottle.
```

**C-FRAME COMPOSITION（六場都貼）**
```
Fixed composition: medium shot, camera at chest height, perfectly level. The bottle stands upright exactly on the right vertical third line, cap top touching the upper third line, logo facing camera. The woman's face sits on the left vertical third line, three-quarter profile facing right. Background fills about 60% of the frame and stays legible, not blurred out. Bottle exposure and color identical across all scenes.
```

**NEGATIVE（負面）**
```
no text, no subtitles, no logo overlays, no watermark, no extra hands, no deformed fingers, no second bottle, no eye contact with camera, no bokeh so strong that the location is unreadable, no lens flare covering the bottle, no color shift on the bottle.
```

## 3.2 角色參考表 Prompt

```
Character reference sheet, one Taiwanese woman late twenties, shoulder-length black hair, natural makeup, neutral expression, front view, three-quarter view, profile view, plus one three-quarter view holding a water bottle in her right hand at chest height. Six wardrobe variations in a row: running wear with damp hair tied up; grey office knit with hair down; hiking windbreaker with cap; linen shirt with wind-blown hair; rain-dampened jacket with hair stuck to cheek; home loungewear. Soft even studio light, plain light grey background, photorealistic, consistent face across all views.
```

## 3.3 逐鏡 Prompt

格式：**IMG** = 關鍵幀圖像 prompt。**VID** = image-to-video 動態 prompt（餵 IMG 結果）。長度都寫進 VID。C 鏡頭的 IMG 記得加 C-FRAME COMPOSITION 區塊。

### 1A 瓶蓋開場
**IMG**
```
Extreme close-up of a thumb pushing open the lid of [PRODUCT], slight 15-degree high angle, pre-dawn deep blue sky as background, soft silver reflector fill from front, 100mm macro, f/4, shallow but bottle cap sharp, dew on the cap, cinematic.
```
**VID**
```
0.8 seconds. Thumb pushes the lid open with a crisp click, tiny water droplet flicks off, camera pushes in 3%, no other motion. Starts from black, fades up over 3 frames.
```

### 1C 河濱 C 格
**IMG**
```
[STYLE][CHARACTER][PRODUCT][C-FRAME COMPOSITION] Riverside bike path in Taipei at dawn, sky gradient from deep blue to orange at the horizon, lamp posts receding, empty path. She wears running wear, hair tied up and damp, chest rising from a run, gaze at the path ahead, not at camera. Sun about to rise behind her at three-quarter back angle, soft white bounce on her face, bottle lit consistently.
```
**VID**
```
2.2 seconds. She is catching her breath, glances forward, then lifts the bottle and drinks two quick sips, lowers it back to the same position. Very slow push-in of about 5% over the clip. Bottle stays on the right third line the whole time. Distant birds, no camera shake.
```

### 1W 河濱大景
**IMG**

```
**VID**
```

```

```

## 3.4 直式 9:16 版

每顆 C 鏡頭 IMG 把 `16:9` 改成 `9:16`，構圖區塊換成：
```
Vertical 9:16 composition, medium shot, camera at chest height, level. The bottle stands upright slightly right of center (about 60% across the frame), cap top at the upper third line, logo facing camera. Her face occupies the upper-left area in three-quarter profile facing right. Background stays legible.
```

## 3.5 音樂 Prompt（Suno / Udio / Higgsfield audio）

```
96 BPM, instrumental, one simple four-bar piano motif in a major key with a slightly wistful sixth chord, repeated without variation for the entire track. Arrangement builds gently underneath: soft felt piano alone, then warm pad, then light acoustic guitar and brushed percussion, then everything drops back to the solo piano motif at 18 seconds, then full silence at 23 seconds, then a short warm piano tail at 27 seconds. No vocals, no melody change, intimate, Taiwanese indie film score feel, 30 seconds.
```

## 3.8 生成順序建議

1. 角色參考表 → 定臉。
2. 用實品照生 TR55 / F60 產品參考圖各 1 張（乾淨、Logo 正面）。
3. 生 1C，確認 C 構圖，把這張當「構圖底圖」。
4. 用 1C 當 reference 依序生 2C、3C、4C、5C、6C，只換背景與服裝描述。六張排一排，壺要對得上一條線。
5. 再生 W、E、A、End。
6. 全部 IMG 過關才開 VID，VID 一律 image-to-video。
7. 剪接：照分鏡表秒數與連環段剪接指令。
